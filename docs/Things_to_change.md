## AFS Stat — Chiikawa Theme Training Actions

| Stat | Chiikawa Theme | Training Action (Clicker / Minigame) | Visual Effects |
|------|---------------|--------------------------------------|----------------|
| **Strength** | Tobatsu (Subduing) | Swinging a Sasumata (the two-pronged fork weapon) | Sweating, swinging bigger forks, hitting training dummies |
| **Durability** | Crying / Resilience | Getting hit by Chimera or standing under a waterfall | Tears shooting out left and right like water guns |
| **Agility / Speed** | Weed Pulling (Kusatori) | Sprinting around ripping weeds out of the ground | Speed trails, pulling massive giant weeds |
| **Special / Energy** | Exam Prep / Intelligence | Studying at a tiny desk with a booklet | Floating text, lightbulbs above head, book stacks piling up |
